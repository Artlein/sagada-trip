THE ITINERARY

Day by Day Schedule

DAY 1 — FRI

Arrival, Paytokan Walk & Sagada Proper

Oct 16, arrival
07:00
Arrive in Sagada, register at Tourist Center
Every visitor registers and pays the mandatory environmental fee at the Tourist Info Center. ₱100/pax
08:00
Luggage Drop-off at Carpenter's Homestay
Drop bags, freshen up, and get ready for the day.
08:30
Early Breakfast / Lunch
Fuel up at local favorites like Yoghurt House, Sagada Brew, or Sagada Lemon Pie House.
10:00
Activity: Paytokan Walk
Guided eco-walk covering 7 sights: Church of St. Mary the Virgin, Sagada Cemetery, Echo Valley, Hanging Coffins, Baw Eng Coffee Farm, Latang Underground River, and Bokong Falls. ₱1,000 / 10 pax
14:30
Check in at Carpenter's Homestay
Official room check-in, hot shower, rest, and change clothes after the hike.
16:00
Roam around Sagada Proper
Explore Ganduyan Museum, Sagada Weaving, Sagada Lemon Pie House / Maggie's Lemon Pie, Sagada Wine Bar, and Yoghurt House.
19:00
Tambay at Cellar Door (Bonfire Place)
Unwind by the open bonfire under pine trees with craft beer and fruit wine.
DAY 2 — SAT

Marlboro & Blue Soil Hike, Cellar Door

Oct 17, full day
04:00
Marlboro Hills and Blue Soil Hills (4-Hour Hiking)
4-hour sunrise hike to Marlboro Hills for the sea of clouds, continuing through the blue-green clay hills of Blue Soil. 4 hrs hiking
12:00
Lunch at Bro Inasal / Gaia Café
Hearty chicken inasal at Bro Inasal or vegetarian specials with terrace views at Gaia Café & Crafts.
15:00
Saturday Sagada Weekend Market
Explore the bustling Saturday market in town proper for fresh mountain produce, local snacks, highland fruits, and artisan handicrafts. Weekend Market
16:30
Free Afternoon Rest & Coffee
Rest your legs back at Carpenter's Homestay or enjoy quiet coffee in town.
18:30
Tambay at Cellar Door (Bonfire Place)
Return to Cellar Door for another cozy night around the pine wood bonfire.
DAY 3 — SUN

Free Day, Souvenirs & Departure

Oct 18, departure
08:00
Free Day & Breakfast
Leisurely morning breakfast and pack bags at Carpenter's Homestay.
10:00
Walk around Sagada Proper & Buy Souvenirs
Shop for pasalubong: Sagada ground Arabica coffee, hand-woven items, mountain tea, and local treats.
12:30
Check out & Lunch
Check out from homestay and enjoy final lunch in town.
14:30
Head to Bus Terminal
Arrive early at the terminal to load luggage.
15:00
3:00 PM Bus to Manila
Board the 3:00 PM bus back to Manila.

SAGADA CULINARY GUIDE

Cafes, Restaurants & Food Spots to Try in Sagada

Scenic Views · Organic

Gaia's Café & Crafts

📍 Ambasing Valley Road 🌱 Vegetarian & Organic

Famous cliffside cafe with sweeping views of the rice terraces and pine forests. Popular spot for wholesome organic dishes, pancakes, and mountain tea.

Pancakes & Pasta Mountain Arabica
Highland BBQ · Popular

Bro's Inasal

📍 Sagada Town Proper 🍗 Grilled Specialties

Go-to spot for savory charcoal-grilled chicken inasal served hot with rice and flavorful chicken oil. Generous portions at affordable local prices.

Chicken Inasal Pork Liempo
Cozy Log Cabin

Misty Lodge & Café

📍 Staiton Road, Sagada 🍕 Burgers & Pizza

Rustic wooden cabin atmosphere serving thick gourmet burgers, stone-baked pizzas, and warm breakfast skillets under pine trees.

Gourmet Burgers Stone-Baked Pizza
Artisan Bakery

Sagada Whole Wheat Homemade Bread

📍 Poblacion, Sagada 🍞 Freshly Baked Daily

Beloved local bakery renowned for fresh, dense whole wheat bread loaves, cinnamon rolls, banana bread, and freshly brewed coffee.

Whole Wheat Loaf Cinnamon Buns
Sagada Institution

Yoghurt House

📍 Town Proper 🥣 Creamy Homemade Yogurt

A Sagada classic operating for decades! Famous for rich, thick homemade yogurt served with wild mountain honey, strawberries, banana, and granola.

Fruit Granola Yogurt Pasta & Mains
Famous Dessert

Sagada Lemon Pie House

📍 South Road, Sagada 🥧 Iconic Pastries

Cozy floor-seating dining area famous for its sweet-tangy lemon pie topped with fluffy toasted meringue. Perfect paired with hot mountain tea.

Signature Lemon Pie Egg Pie & Tea
Fireside Night Spot

Sagada Cellar Door

📍 Lita Avenue, Sagada 🔥 Bonfire & Craft Drinks

Magical evening hangout in a pine grove centered around an open campfire. Serves Sagada craft beers, local fruit wines, sausage, and cheese boards.

Craft Beers Fruit Wine & Bonfire
Traditional Igorot Dish

Pinikpikan House

📍 Sagada Town Proper 🍲 Cordilleran Heritage Food

Authentic spot to sample Cordilleran cuisine! Features Pinikpikan — the iconic native chicken soup prepared with traditional smoked cured pork (etag).

Native Pinikpikan Smoked Etag
